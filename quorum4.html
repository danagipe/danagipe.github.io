The Enemy Within

By Sam-I-Am, Sarenith 13, 319

In the grand and storied history of humankind there have been a rare few inflection points upon which the entire trajectory of our species (and sister-species) has been radically altered; where but for the swift and radical actions of a few far-seeing actors we might instead have been reduced to enslavement, primitive barbarism or outright extinction.

It is my sincere belief, dear reader, that such a time is upon us now.

Already I can detect your incredulity. Surely I am being hyperbolic. Let me assure you that this is a conclusion at which I have not arrived lightly, and I am quite serious when I say that this matter may very well make the difference between our continued prosperity or utter destruction. For long-time readers of this column, who know already of my impassioned soundings of alarm at the lack of resolve demonstrated by our leaders as we wage war against The Swarm, it should come as no surprise that it is this selfsame threat which I fear may be our destruction. Yet in light of recent events I feel compelled to pen anew a fresh warning that I hope will be a clarion call to those who love the Pact Worlds and wish to see us prosper. For I have produced from recent events an alarming theory which if proven true would represent a stark escalation in our current conflict. Indeed, it would mean that agents of the enemy have already infiltrated our neighborhoods, our institutions and even our military.

I speak of the shirren.

“Racist! Xenophobe!” I hear the incredulous cry. Dear reader, do not fall into the trap of such thought-terminating cliches. Rather listen first to my reasons, and come to your own conclusions as to their soundness.

Let us begin chronologically, with the disastrous defense of Ilemchuuva. As we now know, the point of failure in that campaign lay within the defenders themselves: myriad simultaneous acts of sabotage occurring within the ranks of the Knights and the colony militia, all coinciding with an enemy attack. What possible reason might these traitors have had to turncoat? Were they nihilistic worshipers of the Devourer, or some forgotten chaos god of old? Were they simply raving lunatics crazed by drift-madness? None of these seem to ring true.

But what if they were shirren? The shirren are, after all, products of the insatiable war machine that is The Swarm. They claim to have broken away from the hivemind, and that may well be true. But is it so far-fetched to believe that The Swarm has developed some way to enfold them again, even across great distances? That perhaps Ilemchuuva was targeted because of its shirren population, which might be leveraged against any who came to defend it? Could it even be the case that the shirren did not break from The Swarm but were rather intentionally released, in order that they might unwittingly infiltrate our ranks?

This is mere speculation, I know, but in dire times such as these, these questions beg answering.

Just this past weekend, an attack was perpetrated upon Absalom Station, the beating heart and capital of our dear Pact Worlds. What’s more, the focus of this assault was the Stormlight Cathedral, a stronghold of our Knights of Golarion. Despite my criticism of their organization, I recognize the role that the Knights have played, however flawed, in defending our system for the past two centuries. Were the Cathedral to fall and its reliquaries plundered, who knows what destruction could be wrought by malicious actors.

In the aftermath of this troubling event, it occurred to me how advantageous it would be for our enemy if this attack had succeeded. Lo and behold, the culprit has been apprehended, and who is it but a lone shirren operative. Coincidence? Perhaps. Or perhaps not.

I understand that there may remain some skeptics among my readership. That is well and good; skepticism is a product of a healthy mind. But to those who are not convinced, I would pose these questions: how many such attacks must occur before your doubts are dissolved? And how many such attacks do you think we can withstand before our defenses are hobbled beyond salvage? If the former is greater than the latter, can you truly afford not to act?

From those who require no more convincing, who already are prepared to answer this clarion call, I hear the question: What is the prescription? The first step is something for which I have long advocated: the repeal of the banishment of the Hellknights, as I believe it is they alone who possess the resolve to carry out the unsavory work ahead. Dear citizen, in this I ask that you do not hesitate. For the love of the Goddess and Old Golarion, for the survival of humanity and all the Pact Worlds, contact your Council Representative now and demand the return of the Hellknights!
